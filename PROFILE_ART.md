# Profile artwork

This profile uses self-hosted SVG artwork rather than third-party stats cards.

- `kashif-ascii.svg` is an animated ASCII portrait generated from Kashif's Memoji.
- `scripts/refresh_profile.py` reads GitHub's public contribution calendar and renders the heatmap and stats panel.
- `.github/workflows/refresh-profile.yml` refreshes those two live-data SVGs daily.

The profile intentionally describes Kashif as a Computer Science student learning AI/ML; it does not claim a professional AI/ML engineering title.
